## What I'm building

[Mysh](https://playmysh.com) is a story game for kids 7 to 12: they invent a hero, steer the adventure and build their own story worlds. Free, no ads, in three languages, made in Canada. I lead the creative side:

- **The story engine.** Every adventure starts from a story card with a promise it has to keep, every chapter ends with paths the kid picks between, and the kid decides when it ends. I write the rules the AI storyteller follows, and run playtest robots that catch stories that drift, repeat themselves or railroad the player.
- **Licensed worlds.** I lead the creative side of our brand partnerships, bringing worlds kids already love into Mysh. I designed how they stay on-brand: a world bible the storyteller must follow, a fixed cast, and robot tests that try to push stories off-brand.
- **Voice, art and music.** Every turn is written, read aloud and illustrated as the kid plays, with characters speaking in their own voices. I set the art direction, the voices and the music.
- **Kid safety.** Everything a kid types and every picture is checked before a child sees it. I tune those checks to stop real harm without blocking imagination: a dragon battle is fine.
